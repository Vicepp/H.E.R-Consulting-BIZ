Program Planning & Management

Strategic Approaches for Purpose-Driven Planning and Management

ENSURING PROGRAMS ARE PLANNED AND MANAGED FOR IMPACT.

Full details

Human-Centered Design

  • Design programs that prioritize and address specific community needs.
  • Align interventions with local values, ensuring cultural relevance.
  • Focus on creating solutions based on community experiences and input.

Implementation Science

  • Use evidence-based methods to improve program adoption and effectiveness.
  • Focus on scalability to ensure programs can grow and expand successfully.
  • Ensure long-term success and sustainability of public health initiatives.

Program Management Tools & Frameworks

  • Implement proven project management tools for efficient program execution.
  • Use structured frameworks to keep programs on schedule and within scope.
  • Ensure programs meet their strategic goals and deliver desired outcomes.

Community-Engaged Approaches

  • Engage community stakeholders to gather their unique insights and perspectives.
  • Co-create solutions that are tailored to the community’s specific needs.
  • Ensure solutions reflect and respect local values and priorities.

03

Program Evaluation & Impact

Quantifying and Understanding the ‘Why’ and ‘How’ of Program Success

MEASURING, COMMUNICATING, AND AMPLIFYING PROGRAM IMPACT.

Full details

Quantitative & Qualitative Storytelling

  • Utilize data to quantify and measure program outcomes and effectiveness.
  • Employ narrative storytelling to convey the human experiences and impact behind the data.
  • Combine both approaches to provide a comprehensive understanding of a program’s overall impact.

Implementation Science in Evaluation

  • Apply evidence-based methods to improve program adoption and implementation.
  • Focus on scalability to ensure programs can expand effectively.
  • Ensure long-term success and sustainability of public health initiatives.

Public Health & Health Outcomes Focus

  • Evaluate programs based on public health awareness and accessibility.
  • Measure tangible health outcomes to assess program effectiveness.
  • Ensure programs contribute to improving public health on measurable scales.

Evaluation at All Phases

  • Provide evaluation services across planning, implementation, and sustainability phases.
  • Ensure continuous improvement by assessing program performance at each stage.
  • Focus on maximizing long-term impact and effectiveness.
