Company Background

HER Consulting LLC was founded to provide expert consultation services to businesses and organizations in education, healthcare, community development, advocacy groups, foundations, and government organizations locally, nationally, and globally. Specializing in strategic planning, project administration, and program evaluation across multiple sectors, our mission is to partner with clients to deliver tailored solutions that drive community wellness development, public health impact, and operational excellence.

By leveraging proven methodologies and evidence-based practices, we enhance program and organizational effectiveness, optimize health outcomes, and provide long-term value to communities by centering the community, ensuring solutions are responsive to their unique needs and aspirations.

Our Founder and Owner

Our Founder
and Owner

Dr. Omolola (Lola) Olatunde, a licensed pharmacist, is the visionary Founder and Principal Consultant of HER Consulting LLC. With nearly a decade of experience in championing community wellness, development and impact, as well as improving public health, Dr. Olatunde has committed her career to making physical & mental healthcare accessible and optimizing community health outcomes. She brings extensive expertise as the Director of Community Engagement and Evaluation at the Purdue University Center for Health Excellence, Quality, and Innovation, and as a Professor in the College of Pharmacy, where she leads interdisciplinary teams in impactful research and public health policy development.

Subject Matter Experts

HER Consulting has collaborated with numerous subject matter experts (SMEs) to deliver the comprehensive expertise required for the diverse programs we support. Our committed team offers full-time strategic and managerial oversight of your programs, with additional staff and SMEs available to ensure the quality, efficiency, and excellence you deserve.

ADEDAYO ADEKOYA, MSc IT & Data Analytics

Dayo Adekoya is an IT expert specializing in eDiscovery, cybersecurity, data analytics, and legal technology. He excels in managing complex projects for pharmaceutical and legal clients, delivering strategic solutions and driving impactful results through innovative technology.

ROBIN COLEMAN, RN-MPH Health Promotion, Program Planning, DEI

Robin Coleman is a seasoned professional with extensive experience in nursing, public health, and diversity, equity and inclusion (DEI) initiatives. She specializes in holistic health promotion, program development, strategic planning and implementation.

TEMITOPE ADEOYE OLENLOA, PhD Equity-Focused Learning & Applied Research Strategy

Dr. Temitope Adeoye Olenloa is an applied researcher advancing equity in education, workforce, and community systems. She designs culturally responsive environments, facilitates planning, and helps organizations turn data into action, centering communities historically excluded from decisions.

OLUSOLA OMISAKIN, PhD Quantitative Analyst & Demographer

Dr. Olusola Omisakin is a quantitative analyst and demographer specializing in population health research. He applies demographic methods, statistical modeling, and data management to translate complex findings into actionable insights for policy, program development, and organizational decision-making.

HADYA SOW, MPH Qualitative Research & Health Communications

With a strong foundation in community engagement and qualitative methodologies, Hadya Sow specializes in translating lived experiences and community and partner perspectives into evidence-based results. She is experienced in utilizing thematic analysis and storytelling to evaluate and elevate health communication products and public health programs.

MOLLY A. NICHOLS, PharmD, MS, MATS SUBSTANCE USE & BEHAVIORAL HEALTH

Dr. Molly Nichols is a pharmacist and health services researcher who specializes in substance use education and research. Her work highlights the voices of people with lived experience and emphasizes evidence-based, person-centered care. She strives to lead with love and create meaningful programming that transforms heads and hearts about people who use drugs.

DARLEESA ROSS, PhD Public Health Advocate and Research

Dr. Darleesa Ross’s experience in an underserved community drives her work in reducing health disparities. She leads research on hypertension, diabetes, and vaccine hesitancy and develops community-based interventions while serving on public health committees to improve health at local, state, and national levels.

SYMONE BAILEY Grassroots Engagement & Reproductive Justice Advocacy

Symone Bailey is an organizer, storyteller, and reproductive justice advocate with deep roots in Indianapolis. She builds community power through trust, bold conversations, and action. Symone centers Black and Brown voices in policy change and program design, especially in areas of safety, housing, and health, and leads engagement efforts that reflect the realities of those most impacted.

JASON A. WARE, PhD Community Planning & Quality of Life Innovation

Dr. Jason Ware is a scholar and strategist who helps communities define and achieve what thriving looks like. His work connects lived experience with evidence-based tools to drive quality-of-life improvements in place-based planning. He brings over a decade of experience in community-led development, higher education equity, and public sector collaboration.
